**PanePilot 1.8.0** – document metadata in the meta wizard.

## New
- **Documents in the meta wizard:** besides music, photos and videos, the meta wizard now shows the metadata of Office files and PDFs. The switch at the top reads “Music | Photos | Videos | Documents”.
  - **Read and write:** DOCX, XLSX, PPTX (also DOCM, XLSM, PPTM), ODT, ODS, ODP, ODG and PDF.
  - **Read-only:** the old Office formats DOC, XLS, PPT, encrypted PDFs and digitally signed PDFs (a change would invalidate the signature; the sidebar explains this).
  - **Editable fields:** title, subject, author, keywords, description, category, language and custom fields. They are written to the usual document properties, which Word and LibreOffice also show under File → Properties; for PDF to the document information and, if present, the XMP metadata.
  - **Shown:** created, modified, last saved by, application, pages, words and PDF version.
  - **Preview:** the first page of a PDF, or the thumbnail stored in an Office file.
  - **Converters:** tag → file name, file name → tag, tag → tag, tag ↔ text file, with new placeholders such as `%author%`, `%subject%`, `%pages%`, `%created%`, `%modified%` and `%application%`.
- **Properties:** the “Metadata” tab also works for Office documents and PDFs, with a preview of the first PDF page.
- **Refresh** is now also available at the bottom of the context menu of the file view.

## Technical
- New bundled libraries: pypdf, olefile and pypdfium2 (PDF preview).

🇩🇪 **Neu in 1.8.0:** Der Meta-Wizard zeigt und bearbeitet jetzt auch die Metadaten von Office-Dateien und PDFs (Titel, Thema, Autor, Schlagwörter, Beschreibung, Kategorie, Sprache und eigene Felder) – mit Vorschau der ersten PDF-Seite. Alte DOC/XLS/PPT-Dateien sowie verschlüsselte und signierte PDFs werden nur gelesen. Außerdem steht „Aktualisieren“ jetzt ganz unten im Rechtsklick-Menü.
