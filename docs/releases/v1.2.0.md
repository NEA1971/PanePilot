**PanePilot 1.2** – MusicBrainz, disc numbers, update check and a seventh language.

## New
- **MusicBrainz** (meta wizard ▸ converters): search the album in the free MusicBrainz database. Album and artist are prefilled from the tags. The result list (up to 50 releases) can be sorted by every column; releases with as many tracks as files are marked green. In the apply window you choose which track and album fields to take over (checkboxes, values editable by double-click), assign MusicBrainz tracks to files like in “text file → tag” (yellow = length differs by more than 5 s) and optionally take the front cover from the Cover Art Archive (max. 1200 × 1200). The year is the year of the first release; MusicBrainz IDs are stored like MusicBrainz Picard does. Values are marked green and written with “Save tag”.
- **Disc numbers …** (meta wizard ▸ converters): by subfolder (CD1, CD2 …), whenever the track number starts again, or manually. The total can go into the disc field (“1/2”) or into a separate DISCTOTAL field.
- **Check for updates** (Help menu, and optionally once a day at startup – off by default, *Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates*). A new AppImage is downloaded next to the running one under its original name; the old file is kept. PanePilot then offers to restart with the new version.
- **Upper Saxon** (Säggssch) is now a bundled language.

## Changed
- ID3 tags: UFID frames (e.g. the MusicBrainz track ID) are now shown and can be edited; MP4 sort fields (`soar`, `soaa` …) are editable.
