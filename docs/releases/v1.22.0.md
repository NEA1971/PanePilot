**PanePilot 1.22.0** – similar images and videos.

## New
- **Duplicates: f) Similar image/video (picture content)** finds the same picture in another size, compression or format (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF) and the same film in another resolution or container. A slider sets the similarity (70–100 %, default 90 %).
  - Videos are compared at 5 points (10–90 % of the running time, via ffmpeg); their duration may differ by at most 2 %. Images are only compared with images, videos with videos.
  - Rotated, mirrored or heavily cropped pictures are not detected; RAW files are not read.
  - The fingerprints are remembered in `~/.cache/PanePilot`, so a further run only reads new or changed files.
  - The result list shows a thumbnail and a new “Resolution” column (with running time for videos). New quick selection **“Keep highest resolution”**.

---

🇩🇪 **Neu in 1.22.0:** Duplikate f) findet ähnliche Bilder und Videos nach dem Bildinhalt – auch in anderer Größe, Kompression oder einem anderen Format. Regler 70–100 %, Vorschaubilder und Auflösung in der Liste, Schnellwahl „Höchste Auflösung behalten“. Die Fingerabdrücke werden gemerkt, der zweite Durchlauf ist schnell.
