**PanePilot 1.10.0** – panels one above the other, and “Duplicate”.

## New
- **Panels one above the other** (View → “Panels one above the other”, Ctrl+Shift+H), as in FreeCommander.
  - Each panel keeps its own sidebar on the left; the button bar lies horizontally between the panels, its arrows point to the other panel.
  - The menu says “Sidebar top/bottom” instead of “left/right”.
  - Panel and sidebar sizes are remembered separately for both arrangements; the choice is kept after a restart.
- **Duplicate** (context menu and Ctrl+D) creates a copy of the selected files and folders in the same folder without asking: `Report.docx` → `Report-Copy.docx`, then `Report-Copy-2.docx` … The copies are selected afterwards. Not available on FTP/SFTP servers.

Thanks for the suggestion in the discussions!

Außerdem gibt es „Duplizieren“ im Rechtsklick-Menü (Strg+D): `Bericht.docx` → `Bericht-Kopie.docx`.
