**PanePilot 2.2.1** – manual search for online movie data also without the overview.

## Fixed
- **Meta wizard, online data “without overview”:** films that were not found could not be searched manually, and there was no way out when none of the offered matches fitted.
  - The **selection list** (several matches) now has a **“Search manually …”** button – enter title, year or an IMDb/TMDB ID; the result replaces the selection. This works with and without the overview.
  - **Without overview**, films that were not found are now offered one after another for a manual search before anything is applied (“Not found (1/3)”, with Skip and Cancel = skip the rest). Series work the same way; the manual search applies to the whole series.

---

🇩🇪 **Behoben in 2.2.1:** Meta-Wizard, Online-Daten „ohne Übersicht“: Das Auswahlfenster bei mehreren Treffern hat jetzt den Knopf „Manuell suchen …“ (auch mit Übersicht). Nicht gefundene Filme bzw. Serien werden ohne Übersicht vor dem Übernehmen nacheinander zur manuellen Suche angeboten („Nicht gefunden (1/3)“, mit Überspringen und Abbrechen).
