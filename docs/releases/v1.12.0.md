**PanePilot 1.12.0** – track editing for MP4.

## New
- **“Tracks …”** in the meta wizard now also works for **MP4, M4V and MOV**, together with MKV in the same dialog.
  - **Language** is changed directly in the file, in seconds. MP4 requires ISO 639-2/T codes, so `ger` becomes `deu`, `fre` becomes `fra` automatically.
  - **Track name** and **Default** need a new file: PanePilot repacks it with ffmpeg without re-encoding (no quality loss), with progress bar and Cancel. Tags, custom fields and cover are carried over; the old file is only replaced after the new one has been checked.
  - **Forced** is not available for MP4 (hardly supported by the format).

🇩🇪 **Neu in 1.12.0:** „Spuren …“ im Meta-Wizard funktioniert jetzt auch bei MP4: Die Sprache wird direkt in der Datei geändert (`ger` wird dabei zu `deu`), Spurname und „Standard“ durch verlustfreies Neupacken – Tags, eigene Felder und Cover bleiben erhalten.
