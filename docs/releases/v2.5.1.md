**PanePilot 2.5.1** – map: finer zoom and its own sidebar field.

## Changed
- **Map zoom with the mouse wheel is much finer:** one wheel step now zooms a quarter level (before: a whole level, which doubled or halved the scale). The map images scale smoothly in between. Also in the Properties window.
- **“Map” is now a field of its own** in the meta wizard sidebar (with the “Open in browser” link), no longer attached to the GPS latitude. Like any field it can be moved or hidden by right-click. Saved sidebar layouts that contain the GPS fields get the map once, right after them.

---

🇩🇪 **Geändert in 2.5.1:** Das Mausrad zoomt die Karte viel feiner (ein Schritt = ¼ Stufe, stufenlos). Gespeicherte Anordnungen mit GPS-Feldern bekommen die Karte einmalig direkt dahinter.
