**PanePilot 1.21.0** – shared sidebar.

## New
- **Shared sidebar** (View → Shared sidebar, also available for the toolbar and a configurable shortcut): instead of one sidebar per panel there is one sidebar on the far left over the full height – side by side as well as one above the other.
  - It always belongs to the **active panel**: click into the other panel and the folder tree shows its folder, favorites open there; view and favorites set stay the same.
  - **Tools** (multi-rename, search, checksums, meta wizard) stay with the panel they were started in until you close them. Folder synchronization works as before.
  - The View menu then has a single “Sidebar” entry; the width is remembered separately for both layouts. Switch it off to get the two sidebars back.

---

🇩🇪 **Neu in 1.21.0:** Ansicht → „Gemeinsame Seitenleiste“: eine Seitenleiste ganz links für beide Fenster, die immer dem aktiven Fenster folgt. Werkzeuge wie Suche oder Umbenennen bleiben beim Fenster, in dem sie gestartet wurden. Ausschalten bringt die zwei Seitenleisten zurück.
