# Sidebars

Each panel can have a sidebar on its outer side. You choose its content via **View ▸ Sidebar** or by right-clicking in the sidebar.

- **Folder tree**: all folders as a tree, with the **drives** above: internal disks, USB disks and sticks, network shares and CD/DVD drives.
  - USB drives appear even before they are mounted; a click mounts and opens them.
  - Right-click: mount, unmount, safely remove.
  - EFI and internal system partitions are deliberately not shown.
- **Favourites**: your remembered folders, with groups. Right-click to rename, edit and delete.
- **Servers**: FTP and SFTP servers, see [Servers](servers.md).
- **Checksums**: see [Checksums](checksums.md).
- **Multi-rename** and **Meta wizard** take one side of the window when needed, see [Multi-rename](rename.md) and [Meta wizard](metawizard.md).

The width of a sidebar can be changed with the mouse; PanePilot remembers it.
