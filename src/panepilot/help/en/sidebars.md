# Sidebars

Each panel can have a sidebar on its outer side. You choose its content via **View ▸ Sidebar** or by right-clicking in the sidebar.

- **Folder tree**: all folders as a tree, with the **drives** above: internal disks, USB disks and sticks, network shares and CD/DVD drives.
  - USB drives appear even before they are mounted; a click mounts and opens them.
  - Right-click: mount, unmount, safely remove.
  - EFI and internal system partitions are deliberately not shown.
- **Favourites**: your remembered folders, with groups. Right-click to rename, edit and delete.
  - **Sort by name** (right-click a favorite folder, or an empty spot for the whole set): sorts everything below it including subfolders – ascending, ignoring case, numbers naturally (“2” before “10”). Separator lines are section borders: sorting happens only within the sections, the lines stay in place. Whether folders or favorites come first is set under Settings → File view → Sorting favorites.
  - **Insert separator** (right-click): a thin line directly below the clicked entry, also inside favourite folders. Drag it like a favourite, remove it with Del. **“Edit …”** gives it an optional name, bold/italic/colour like a favourite and the alignment left, centred or right; with a colour the line is coloured too. The line starts where the favourite texts start and ends after the longest favourite.
- **FTP servers**: FTP and SFTP servers, see [FTP servers](servers.md).
- **Checksums**: see [Checksums](checksums.md).
- **Multi-rename** and **Meta wizard** take one side of the window when needed, see [Multi-rename](rename.md) and [Meta wizard](metawizard.md).

The width of a sidebar can be changed with the mouse; PanePilot remembers it.
